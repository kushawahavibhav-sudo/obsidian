Scroll — the phone opens

A procedurally built 3D smartphone. As you scroll, the back panel unlatches, slides down and swings open on a side hinge. The battery lifts out and slides away, then the motherboard separates with its chips and camera module layers. The background then turns to blueprint paper and the phone becomes line art, with labels pointing at parts: SoC for Linux and systems, camera for computer vision, battery for optimization, board for Android internals and custom ROMs, display for web and frontend, antenna for networking, and memory for local LLMs.
